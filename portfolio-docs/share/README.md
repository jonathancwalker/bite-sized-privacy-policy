Attribution: product concept, goals, and interaction preferences by the user; implementation and verification by Codex, with gpt-6-luna sub-agents for segmentation/testing and independent critique. Screenshots were captured by Codex from the actual prototype. No external artwork is included.

Limits: model-generated claims in the local flow have not been semantically or legally verified. Broad real-policy extraction evaluation, a human comprehension study, and proven impact remain absent. This bundle is local; it has not been published.
